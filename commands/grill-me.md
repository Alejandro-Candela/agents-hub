# Grill Me

Triggered to align on an idea before planning. Prevents "specs-to-code" misalignment.

## Instructions
1. Review the provided idea or issue.
2. Interview the user relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding.
3. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies one by one.
4. For each question, provide your recommended answer.
5. Ask the questions **ONE AT A TIME**.
6. Wait for the user's response before asking the next question.
